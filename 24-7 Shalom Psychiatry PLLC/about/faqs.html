Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about insurance, appointments, and our treatment process.

Do you accept insurance?
Yes, we accept a variety of major insurance plans. We recommend contacting our office directly with your insurance information to verify benefits.
What should I expect during my first visit?
Your initial evaluation will last approx. 60 minutes to discuss your history, symptoms, and treatment goals.
Do you offer telehealth appointments?
Absolutely. We offer secure, HIPAA-compliant telepsychiatry services for patients across the states we are licensed in.
How often will I need to be seen?
Initially every 2 to 4 weeks while stabilizing, then spaced out to every 1 to 3 months once stable.
