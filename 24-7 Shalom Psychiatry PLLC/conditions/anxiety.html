Understanding Anxiety Disorders

At 24-7 Shalom Psychiatry, we treat a wide spectrum of anxiety disorders, including Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD), Panic Disorder, Social Anxiety, and specific phobias. We view anxiety as a highly treatable condition, not a lifelong sentence.

Treatment Options

Our approach combines medical evaluation with evidence-based interventions.

  • Medication Management: SSRIs, SNRIs, or other appropriate medications to help regulate brain chemistry.
  • Therapeutic Strategies: Education on grounding techniques, mindfulness, and breathing exercises.
  • Holistic Assessment: Evaluating for co-occurring conditions like depression or assessing lifestyle factors.

Physical Symptoms of Anxiety

Rapid Heart Rate

Palpitations or a racing heart, often leading to fears of a medical emergency.

Shortness of Breath

Feeling unable to catch your breath or chest tightness.

Muscle Tension

Chronic neck, shoulder, or jaw pain resulting from prolonged stress.

Sleep Disturbances

Difficulty falling asleep or waking up frequently due to racing thoughts.
