The Mind-Body Connection

Living with chronic pain can be exhausting, leading to depression, anxiety, and severe sleep disturbances. At 24-7 Shalom Psychiatry, we focus on the psychiatric and behavioral aspects of pain management, working alongside your primary care or pain specialists.

Our Behavioral Approach

We do not prescribe interventional pain management medications (like opioids), but rather focus on psychiatric interventions that support pain patients.

  • Psychiatric Medication: Utilizing specific antidepressants or nerve-pain modifying agents (like SNRIs or Gabapentinoids) that have dual benefits for mood and pain.
  • Sleep Architecture: Treating the underlying insomnia to restore restorative sleep phases.
  • Mind-Body Techniques: Education on how the nervous system processes pain and how to calm the sympathetic nervous system.

Common Challenges We Address

Pain-Induced Depression

Feelings of hopelessness, loss of interest in activities, and emotional fatigue resulting from persistent pain.

Anxiety & Stress

Constant worry about flare-ups, which can ironically increase muscle tension and worsen the pain.

Sleep Disturbances

The cycle of pain preventing restful sleep, which in turn lowers your pain threshold the next day.
